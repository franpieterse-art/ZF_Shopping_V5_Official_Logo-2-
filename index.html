Enter up to 15 grocery items • Compare 3 retailers • Open their delivery service

Find the best total grocery price.

A phone-friendly shopping comparison prototype for South Africa.

Demo / manual price mode: This app does not currently fetch live retailer prices or stock. Enter verified prices for the three retailers below. Results are calculated from your entered prices only; unavailable items make a basket incomplete.

1. Your shopping list (0/15)

Use consistent units (e.g. 1 kg, 18 eggs) to make prices comparable.

2. Enter current shelf prices (R)

Use each supplier's website, app or catalogue to check prices. Leave blank if an item is unavailable or not verified.

3. Best shopping option

Delivery links open the retailer's own website or delivery platform. Availability, delivery charges, stock and checkout prices must be confirmed there. Basket items are not automatically transferred.
